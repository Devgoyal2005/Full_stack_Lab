Outpatient Department (OPD)

The Outpatient Department (OPD) is the first point of contact for patients seeking medical care at City Hospital. It provides consultation, diagnosis, and treatment without the need for hospital admission.

Our OPD is designed to offer a comfortable and efficient healthcare experience, supported by experienced doctors, nursing staff, and modern medical facilities.

Facilities Available

  • General Physician Consultation
  • Specialist Clinics (Cardiology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics)
  • Prescription and Follow-ups
  • Health Checkup Packages
  • Patient Registration and Records

Working Hours

Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
Sunday: Emergency consultations only
