Emergency Zone

The Emergency Zone at City Hospital is dedicated to providing immediate medical care for critical and life-threatening conditions. It operates round the clock to handle medical emergencies efficiently.

The department is staffed with experienced emergency physicians, nurses, and support teams who are trained to respond quickly under high-pressure situations.

Emergency Services

  • 24x7 Emergency Medical Care
  • Ambulance and Trauma Support
  • Critical Care and Stabilization
  • Immediate Diagnostic Access
  • Disaster and Accident Response

Availability

Open: 24 Hours a Day
Emergency Helpline: Available at all times
