Diagnostic Laboratory

The Diagnostic Laboratory at City Hospital plays a vital role in accurate medical diagnosis by providing reliable and timely test results. It supports doctors in identifying health conditions at early stages.

The laboratory is equipped with modern diagnostic equipment and is managed by trained technicians and pathologists to ensure precision and safety in every test conducted.

Laboratory Services

  • Blood and Urine Analysis
  • Biochemistry and Pathology Tests
  • X-Ray and Imaging Services
  • ECG and Diagnostic Screening
  • Health Checkup Reports

Working Hours

Monday to Saturday: 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM
Sunday: Limited services available
